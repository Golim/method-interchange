Claim 4 — End-to-end crawl and method-interchangeability workflow

MIDE's browser crawler discovers linked pages, fills and submits POST forms, and
records the resulting requests and responses. The post2get command converts and
replays supported requests as GET candidates, and analyze classifies route
acceptance and response equivalence.

The Compose-only Flask target is deterministic and local. It covers URL-encoded,
multipart, and JSON bodies, with GET endpoints that match POST responses, reject
GET, or accept GET with a different application-level `result`. A
parameter-dependent endpoint also checks that converted data reaches an otherwise
valid GET route.

This claim exercises MIDE's pipeline against a controlled target; it does not
reproduce Internet-scale measurements from the paper.
