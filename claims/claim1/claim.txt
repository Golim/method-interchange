Claim 1 — Baseline-controlled detection

MIDE's offline analysis distinguishes strong HTTP method interchangeability from
generic endpoint responses by comparing a converted GET both with the captured POST
and with a no-query GET control. The supplied fixture exercises all four classes:
10 A (strong interchangeability), 8 B (ambiguous), 6 C (baseline collision), and
6 D (not interchangeable) records across form-urlencoded, JSON, and multipart
bodies.

This is an executable workflow demonstration, not a reproduction of the paper's
Internet-scale prevalence counts.
