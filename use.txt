INTENDED USE

MIDE is intended for authorized research on HTTP method interchangeability. The
bundled synthetic fixture and all claim scripts are safe and offline.

LIMITATIONS AND SAFETY

Live crawling, replay, CSRF token checks, and WCD checks can contact remote systems
and may trigger server-side behavior. Run them only with explicit authorization from
the system owner. Conservative default filters and rate limits do not replace that
authorization. Results from live targets are time-dependent.

The archive does not include the raw Internet-scale measurement corpus because it
may contain target-specific or sensitive request/response material. It therefore
demonstrates the software workflow but cannot regenerate the paper's exact aggregate
counts without a separately reviewed sanitized data release.
